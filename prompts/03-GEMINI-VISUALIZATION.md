# Gemini — Visualize Approved Architecture

Use only after a plan is owner-approved and audited. In a Gemini Gem/project, attach the common rules, property brief, approved ground/first/site/elevation plans and style board once.

For each image, paste:

```text
Use the approved dimensioned plans as immutable geometry and the style board for appearance only.

Create ONE [VIEW] of [ROOM / EXTERIOR] from [CAMERA POSITION]. Preserve plot orientation, roads, gates, room boundaries, dimensions, doors, windows, stairs, levels, light courts and service stacks. Do not add, remove, mirror, rotate, resize or relocate architecture.

Apply a contemporary climate-responsive Telangana family-home style: warm off-white plaster, restrained grey-beige stone, medium teak, muted terracotta jaali, charcoal metal, matte cream flooring, muted sage textiles, restrained brass pooja details, linen curtains and local planting. Domestic and practical—not hotel, resort, palace or apartment block.

Use realistic Indian-scale furniture and keep every access route clear. If styling conflicts with geometry, preserve geometry. Before returning the image, list anything you could not preserve.
```

Replace `[VIEW]` with one of:

- realistic top-down furnished plan;
- north-west 3D cutaway;
- full G+1 exploded cutaway;
- west-road exterior;
- north-entrance exterior;
- south-garden exterior;
- eye-level interior;
- evening exterior.

For the Mallanna room append:

```text
Preserve the approved raised platform/table, four decorated non-structural pandal pillars, prasadam space and clear space for four people. No Patnam inside. Use a family-approved deity reference or neutral placeholder; do not invent a deity likeness.
```

Reject an image if it changes geometry, orientation, household independence, room count, pooja separation, kitchen/master zones, parking/access or approved openings.
