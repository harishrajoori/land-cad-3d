# GPT-6 Astra — Design a Property

In Kiro, common requirements and Vaastu knowledge load automatically. Usually a one-line request is enough:

```text
Design [PROPERTY FILE or DIMENSIONS + ROAD SIDE + DESIRED DOOR FACING].

Internally generate and score at least three materially different options using the auto-loaded owner brief, common design rules, decision log, Vaastu knowledge and review checklist. Self-audit the strongest option and correct blockers/majors before responding.

Return one best complete architecture concept in the first response: assumptions, site/footprint, ground and first floors, room dimensions, adjacency/access directions, doors/windows, furniture logistics, parking/external stair, daylight/ventilation, structural/service stacking, roof/elevation intent, Vaastu matrix, north-up dimensioned plan artifact, and professional hold points. Summarize the other options and why they ranked lower.

Do not reuse old generated plans, silently change fixed requirements, create photoreal images, or claim professional certification.
```

Examples:

- `Design properties/PROPERTY-1-WEST-80x50.md.`
- `Design a 40 × 60 ft rectangular plot in Karimnagar with a 20 ft west road and north-facing house door.`
