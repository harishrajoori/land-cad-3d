# Architecture Design Acceptance Checklist

Use once per option. Evidence must come from dimensions/geometry, not attractive images or unsupported prose.

## Site and legal
- [ ] Surveyed polygon and true north attached.
- [ ] Roads, measured widths, levels and widening lines recorded.
- [ ] Authority jurisdiction/title/layout status recorded.
- [ ] Setbacks, coverage, floors/height and parking labelled verified or provisional.

## Programme
- [ ] Latest owner requirements used; no stale 3BHK/two-car/Patnam assumptions.
- [ ] Ground: 2 bedrooms + office; separate daily and Mallanna pooja.
- [ ] First household access independent; first-floor programme explicitly stated.
- [ ] One car + two bikes; garden/play/open space retained.

## Access and daily use
- [ ] Exactly one intended ground main house door; facing and route are explicit.
- [ ] Every room reachable from common circulation; no through-bedroom access.
- [ ] Common bath convenient to Bedroom 2/office and private from entry/dining/pooja.
- [ ] Master → dressing → attached bath remains private.
- [ ] Doors/windows do not collide; lobbies/passages have clear widths.
- [ ] Furniture delivery and installed furniture clearance checked.
- [ ] Parking, pedestrian and stair gates/routes do not conflict.

## Vaastu
- [ ] Surveyed north/reference outline used.
- [ ] Pooja NE, kitchen SE, master SW substantially—not labels only.
- [ ] Bedroom 2 W/NW and office N/NW reviewed.
- [ ] Toilets avoid NE/centre; exact sub-zones marked CONSULT.
- [ ] Pooja does not share bathroom wall or direct opposing door.
- [ ] Exact entrance pada marked CONSULT until selected system/consultant.
- [ ] Mallanna shrine and people/offerings fit without overlap.

## Climate and services
- [ ] Every habitable room has useful daylight/ventilation.
- [ ] Kitchen/toilet exhaust and service shafts are explicit.
- [ ] West-facing heat controlled with shade/material/planting.
- [ ] Kitchens and wet areas stack logically in G+1.
- [ ] No toilet above ground pooja where avoidable.
- [ ] Roof drainage, tank, solar and stair headroom coordinated.

## Structure and safety
- [ ] Provisional column/grid concept aligns both floors.
- [ ] Long spans/openings identified for engineer.
- [ ] Stair width, rise/run, landings, railing and headroom reviewed.
- [ ] Foundation/soil/seismic/electrical/fire items are professional hold points.

## Decision status
- [ ] BLOCKER/MAJOR/MINOR/HOLD findings recorded.
- [ ] Owner-fixed versus engineer-adjustable elements listed.
- [ ] One revision identifier used across plans/images/schedules.
- [ ] Images checked against geometry before acceptance.
