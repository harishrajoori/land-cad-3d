# Telugu / Telangana Vaastu Knowledge Guide

> **Supplementary implementation checklist.** The canonical project research and latest owner brief are in [`VAASTU-KNOWLEDGE.md`](VAASTU-KNOWLEDGE.md). If these files differ on family requirements, source interpretation, or plot application, `VAASTU-KNOWLEDGE.md` takes precedence.

**Status:** researched design knowledge, not a construction approval or a certified Vaastu opinion
**Prepared for:** residential planning in Karimnagar, Telangana
**Last reviewed:** 24 September 2026

## 1. Purpose and limits

Use this guide to create, compare, and review house concepts before taking the selected option to a licensed architect/engineer and the family's Vaastu consultant.

There is no single government-issued standard called “Telugu Vaastu” or “Telangana Vaastu.” Regional Telugu practice generally applies the wider **Vastu Purusha Mandala** tradition, with family- and consultant-specific interpretations. Published practitioners disagree on exact entrance padas, toilet sub-zones, water/septic positions, and some facing-specific details. This document therefore separates:

- **CONSENSUS:** repeatedly stated high-level zoning; suitable as a default design preference.
- **PREFERENCE:** common practice, but trade-offs are expected.
- **CONSULT:** select and record the family's consultant-specific rule before finalising.
- **LEGAL:** statutory/engineering requirement; always overrides Vaastu preferences.

Vaastu is a cultural/traditional planning system, not a substitute for structural engineering, fire and life safety, ventilation, sanitation, accessibility, climate design, title verification, or municipal approval.

## 2. Order of precedence for every design

Apply constraints in this order:

1. **LEGAL:** title/layout status, surveyed boundary, road widening, TG-bPASS/Karimnagar rules, setbacks, height/floor limits, parking, rainwater harvesting, fire and sanitation requirements.
2. **SAFETY/ENGINEERING:** structure, soil/foundation, stair safety, electrical/fire safety, plumbing and drainage.
3. **FUNCTION:** usable room dimensions, privacy, circulation, daylight, ventilation, accessibility and family ritual requirements.
4. **VAASTU:** directional zoning and selected consultant rules.
5. **STYLE:** elevation, finishes, furniture and decoration.

Never make a plan unsafe, illegal, dark, unventilated, or unusable merely to satisfy a simplified nine-cell diagram.

## 4. Establishing north and “facing” correctly

### ORIENT-01 — Surveyed north (LEGAL + CONSENSUS)

- Use the **survey drawing / true north bearing**, not the top of a broker's brochure and not an assumed sunrise direction.
- Store north as an angle in the project. Do not rotate north when the camera or drawing rotates.
- For irregular plots, calculate zones from the surveyed plot/building geometry and document the chosen centre. Four side lengths alone do not prove the shape.

### ORIENT-02 — Definition of house facing (CONSULT)

A common convention defines facing by standing inside at the main door and looking outward. Thus, looking toward the west makes it a west-facing entrance. [Astroyogi describes this convention for west-facing houses](https://www.astroyogi.com/vastu/west-facing-house-in-vastu-shastra).

Road side and house facing are not automatically identical:

- A corner plot can have roads on two sides but only one selected main entrance.
- Record plot roads, gate, pedestrian entrance, car gate and house main door separately.
- Do not label a plot “north-facing” solely because it has a north road.

## 5. Core zone rules

### V-NE-01 — North-East / Ishanyam (CONSENSUS)

**Prefer:** pooja/meditation, clean light area, garden/open space, or water-related preference after engineering review.
**Avoid:** toilet, septic tank, staircase, heavy store, major structural mass, clutter.
**Design check:** assess the room's actual footprint, not merely whether its label or centroid falls inside NE.

### V-SE-01 — South-East / Agneyam (CONSENSUS)

**Prefer:** kitchen and controlled fire/heat functions.
**Common alternative:** North-West kitchen when SE cannot work.
**Practical rules:** give the stove and sink separation; provide daylight, exhaust and a safe LPG/electrical layout. Cooking facing east is a common preference, not a legal requirement.

### V-SW-01 — South-West / Nairuthi (CONSENSUS)

**Prefer:** master bedroom, secure storage, heavier/taller building mass.
**Avoid:** large openings that destroy privacy and west/south heat control; underground water preference; main pooja.
Published west-facing guidance consistently places the master in SW and keeps NE lighter, including [DesignCafe's west-facing overview](https://www.designcafe.com/blog/home-interiors/west-facing-house-vastu/) and [its west-facing plan guide](https://www.designcafe.com/blog/home-interiors/west-facing-house-vastu-plan-30x40/).

### V-NW-01 — North-West / Vayavyam (PREFERENCE)

**Often used for:** guest bedroom, common toilet/bath, utility, parking or movement-related uses.
**Caution:** exact toilet placement is disputed; use the toilet procedure below.

### V-C-01 — Brahmasthanam / centre (CONSENSUS)

**Prefer:** circulation, visual openness, courtyard/light well or low-obstruction family space.
**Avoid:** toilet, septic, heavy stair/core, large columns or permanent storage where practicable.
“Open” does not require wasting the entire centre; a well-lit hall or circulation zone can preserve function on a compact plot.

### V-NE-SW-01 — Weight and openness gradient (PREFERENCE)

- Keep north/east/NE comparatively open, lower and lighter.
- Keep south/west/SW comparatively heavier, higher and more enclosed.
- More openings toward north/east and better shading toward west/south is also useful climate design for Karimnagar; it should be justified by daylight and heat control, not superstition alone.

### Toilet and bathroom procedure (important conflict area)

Modern sources disagree: some recommend NW/West, while 16-zone systems prefer narrower disposal sub-zones such as WNW, SSW or ESE. Compare [Architectural Digest's warning against NE toilets](https://www.architecturaldigest.in/story/toilet-directions-as-per-vastu-do-toilets-in-the-north-east-direction-bring-bad-luck/), [Ongrid's discussion of SSW/WNW/ESE](https://ongrid.design/blogs/home-building-guide/vastu-passive-architecture-toilet-location), and the different NW-oriented recommendation summarised by [Digit](https://www.godigit.com/vastu-shastra/vastu-remedies-for-washroom).

Use this policy:

1. **Hard avoid in the design tool:** NE and Brahmasthanam.
2. **Candidate zones:** NW/W initially, or exact WNW/SSW/ESE when a 16/32-zone consultant profile is selected.
3. **Do not hardcode SW or SE as universally good/bad** without selecting a named consultant rule set.
4. Stack wet areas sensibly between G and first floors; plumbing, waterproofing and ventilation are mandatory.

## 7. Entrance rules and padas

### ENT-01 — Do not equate “north/east good” with a valid door (CONSULT)

Entrance advice is highly school-specific. The wall is divided into padas or finer entrance zones; counting direction and names differ between 8/9-pada and 32-zone systems. Therefore:

- Store entrance position as wall + measured offset + compass angle.
- Do not let the app declare an entrance compliant from cardinal facing alone.
- Require consultant confirmation before the design is marked “final Vaastu reviewed.”

### ENT-W-01 — West-facing plot (PREFERENCE + CONSULT)

- A west-facing home is not inherently inauspicious; room zoning and the exact entrance sector matter.
- Use the west-to-north-west portion as the **initial candidate band**, while avoiding the south-west extreme until reviewed.
- One published convention places a west entrance in the 3rd–6th padas counted from NW; do not treat that numbering as universal. See [Astroyogi's west-facing guidance](https://www.astroyogi.com/vastu/west-facing-house-in-vastu-shastra) and [DesignCafe's warning to identify the chosen entrance pada before fixing the layout](https://www.designcafe.com/blog/home-interiors/west-facing-house-vastu-plan-30x40/).

### ENT-N/E/S-01 — Other facings (PREFERENCE + CONSULT)

- North: start with a north/NE candidate band.
- East: start with an east/NE candidate band.
- South: a south-facing home can be planned, but use the consultant's exact pada; do not reject the plot from direction alone. Published south-facing guides also treat door position as critical rather than declaring every south-facing home invalid.

Keep the pedestrian gate, car gate and house door separately adjustable. A gate aligned directly with the main door is a consultant preference, not a structural requirement.

## 8. G+1 and two independent households

### FLOOR-01 — Repeat and stack intelligently

- Evaluate Vaastu for each floor while preserving the whole-building hierarchy.
- Stack kitchens/wet areas where practical to simplify services.
- Avoid a toilet directly above the principal pooja/shrine where possible.
- Maintain structural wall/column continuity; never move upper walls solely for Vaastu without engineering review.

### FLOOR-02 — Independent first-floor access

For this project, the brother's first-floor home must remain reachable while the ground-floor home is locked:

- Give the stair an external entrance and independent landing.
- Prefer S/W/SW placement, but prioritise safe rise/run, landing, headroom and evacuation.
- Do not let the external stair block the main entrance, car turning, ventilation, outdoor Patnam or emergency access.
- Independently review the upstairs main door's direction and exact pada.

## 9. Plot, roads, slope, water and services

### SITE-01 — Shape and geometry

- Square/rectangular plots are traditionally preferred; irregular plots require a measured polygon and professional review.
- Do not infer a corner plot from frontage/depth alone. Record every road side and width.
- Check corner splays, road-widening lines, easements and utility reservations before establishing the buildable envelope.

### SITE-02 — Ground level and drainage (PREFERENCE + ENGINEERING)

Traditional guidance often prefers SW higher/heavier and NE lower/open. Apply only if compatible with:

- verified road/plot levels;
- storm-water discharge and flood risk;
- foundation and soil design;
- neighbour and municipal drainage requirements.

Never direct water onto neighbouring land or create a low NE pocket that floods.

### SITE-03 — Water/septic (CONSULT + ENGINEERING)

Common traditions prefer borewell/underground water toward N/NE/E and overhead tank/heavy storage toward S/W/SW. Septic and toilet sub-zones vary substantially. Final positions require:

- Vaastu consultant's selected rule set;
- soil/percolation and groundwater review;
- setbacks from foundations, wells and property lines;
- municipal water/sewer requirements.

## 10. Mallanna pooja and outdoor Patnam — project-specific rules

These are **family ritual requirements**, not universal Telugu-Vaastu rules. Family/purohit instructions override generic internet layouts.

### MALLANNA-01 — Indoor room

- Prefer NE with the daily pooja when space permits.
- Required elements: raised shrine platform, table-like surface, decorated four-pillar pandal, prasadam space in front, and clear worship space for four people in addition to the shrine footprint.
- Keep the four pandal pillars distinct from structural columns.
- Confirm deity/image dimensions, facing, platform height, lamp/incense ventilation, storage, and whether four people includes the pujari.

### MALLANNA-02 — Compact-plot adaptation

When space is constrained:

1. remove decorative furniture before shrinking circulation;
2. reduce the Mallanna room toward an agreed usable minimum;
3. merge the separate daily pooja into the Mallanna pooja room;
4. never overlap the shrine/pillars, prasadam area and four-person clear worship area;
5. report the compromise visibly.

### PATNAM-01 — Outdoor ritual area

- Patnam remains outside, in front of the home; “front” follows the selected main entrance, not a permanently fixed compass direction.
- Keep a measurable clear rectangle plus circulation around it.
- Do not double-count the same area for simultaneous Patnam, car parking and stair access. If parking must move temporarily on ritual days, record that operational decision.

The [Government of India's Komuravelli Mallanna Jaathara reference](https://utsav.gov.in/view-event/komuravelli-mallanna-jaathara) documents Oggu Pujaris creating Patnam during Mallanna worship. It does not prescribe domestic room dimensions; this family's ritual practice and pujari guidance govern the home design.

## 11. Karimnagar climate and practical architecture

Treat these as performance requirements alongside Vaastu:

- Shade west-facing walls, windows and sit-outs with deep overhangs, verandahs, fins, trees or screened balconies.
- Place frequently used rooms for useful daylight and cross-ventilation; do not make a room dark merely to force its label into one zone.
- Use operable windows on different orientations where possible.
- Vent kitchens and toilets directly outdoors or through designed shafts.
- Protect the roof/terrace from heat and water ingress; coordinate solar PV, tank and stair headroom.
- Design the main entrance, ground-floor bedroom and bathroom for elderly/step-free access if relevant.

## 12. Small-plot adaptation policy

Never uniformly scale a large plan down. Use this decision ladder and show every adaptation:

1. Preserve legal setbacks, safe stair, ventilation and essential circulation.
2. Drop or simplify furniture that does not fit; furniture is not architecture.
3. Combine living and dining while keeping usable circulation.
4. Reduce the Mallanna room only to the family-approved minimum.
5. Merge daily pooja into Mallanna pooja when necessary.
6. Reduce parking from one car + bikes to bikes-only when the owner accepts it.
7. Move nonessential store/dressing/utility to another floor or combine it.
8. If bedrooms, kitchen, bathrooms or safe circulation fall below usable minimums, mark the plot **INFEASIBLE FOR THIS BRIEF** instead of generating a misleading image.

Project planning targets (not law and not Vaastu scripture):

| Space | Compact clear target | Comfortable target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secondary bedroom | about 10 × 11 ft | 11 × 12 or 12 × 12 ft |
| Master bedroom | about 11 × 12 ft | 12 × 14 or 13 × 15 ft |
| Kitchen | about 8 × 10 ft | 9 × 11 or larger |

## 13. Telangana/Karimnagar statutory preflight (LEGAL)

Vaastu does not grant building permission. Before evaluating room layouts, obtain or verify:

- registered title and approved/layout-regularised plot status;
- survey polygon, north bearing and all road widths;
- proposed/recorded road widening;
- land use and local authority jurisdiction;
- applicable front/rear/side setbacks, coverage, height and number of floors;
- parking and access requirements;
- rainwater-harvesting, sewer/septic, water and utility requirements;
- architect/engineer responsibilities, sanctioned plan and occupancy process.

The legal framework includes the [Telangana State Building Permission Approval and Self-Certification System Act, 2020 (Act 12 of 2020)](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/telangana/2020/Act12of2020TG.pdf) and the Common Building Rules originally issued under G.O.Ms.No.168 with amendments. [G.O.Ms.No.52 dated 12 March 2022](https://tgbpass.telangana.gov.in/html/docs/go_s_and_act/G.O.Ms.No%2052,%20dt.12.03.2022.pdf) describes the Common Building Rules as applying across municipal corporations, municipalities, UDAs and covered rural areas, together with TS-bPASS rules. [G.O.Ms.No.7 dated 5 January 2016](https://tgbpass.telangana.gov.in/html/docs/go_s_and_act/3_GOMsNo_7_MA_UD_dt_05012016.PDF) is one amendment in that rules history.

TG-bPASS procedure varies by plot/building category. Published summaries cite special tracks for very small plots and self-certification up to 500 m² / 10 m, but amendments and local facts may change the applicable route. Verify the current process on the [official TG-bPASS portal](https://tgbpass.telangana.gov.in/) and with Karimnagar Municipal Corporation rather than encoding those thresholds as permanent design constants.

The official [TG-bPASS occupancy/site checklist](https://tgbpass.telangana.gov.in/html/docs/checklist_%20OC_Site_Checklist.pdf) checks actual floors, building height, front/rear/side setbacks, parking, road widening and rainwater-harvesting pits. This demonstrates why the sanctioned geometry—not an AI image—must remain the approval source of truth. The [Karimnagar district portal](https://karimnagar.telangana.gov.in/) confirms the local government context; obtain the exact municipal/planning authority for the surveyed site.

## 15. Design review checklist

### Inputs

- [ ] Surveyed boundary/polygon and true north attached.
- [ ] Every road side and measured road width recorded.
- [ ] Main door, pedestrian gate and vehicle gate treated separately.
- [ ] Current legal buildable envelope verified; no invented setbacks.
- [ ] Family brief and compact-plot trade-offs confirmed.

### Ground floor

- [ ] Kitchen footprint substantially occupies SE or approved alternative.
- [ ] Master footprint substantially occupies SW.
- [ ] Pooja/Mallanna substantially occupies NE and is not merely labelled there.
- [ ] Toilet avoids NE and centre; selected sub-zone rule recorded.
- [ ] Brahmasthan is not blocked by toilet, septic, heavy stair or storage.
- [ ] Every room is reachable without walking through another bedroom.
- [ ] Daylight, ventilation and furniture clearance checked.

### G+1

- [ ] Independent first-floor stair works while ground home is locked.
- [ ] Safe stair width, rise/run, landing and headroom reviewed.
- [ ] Wet areas stack sensibly; no toilet directly above principal pooja where avoidable.
- [ ] Upper walls/columns align structurally.
- [ ] Each household has privacy and independent services/access decisions recorded.

### Site and rituals

- [ ] Parking works with actual vehicle dimensions and turning path.
- [ ] Patnam does not overlap parking/stair/entrance during intended use.
- [ ] Mallanna platform/pandal/prasadam/four-person clear area do not overlap.
- [ ] Garden/play area is genuinely usable, not leftover strips only.
- [ ] West façade shading and cross-ventilation reviewed for Karimnagar climate.

### Final discussion pack

- [ ] Site plan, both floor plans, room schedule and 3D views share one revision ID.
- [ ] Vaastu exceptions and consultant decisions are listed explicitly.
- [ ] Legal/engineering unknowns are visible, not silently assumed.
- [ ] Attractive AI renders are checked against the dimensioned model before decisions.

## 16. Source quality and maintenance

This is a synthesis, not a verbatim reproduction. Government sources are authoritative only for the legal material they contain; lifestyle/architecture sources document common contemporary Vaastu interpretations, not statutory or universally agreed rules. When two sources conflict, this guide marks the rule **CONSULT** rather than choosing one silently.

Review this document when:

- the family selects a specific Vaastu consultant or Telugu reference text;
- surveyed north or plot geometry changes;
- TG-bPASS/Common Building Rules are amended;
- Karimnagar authority confirms plot-specific setbacks/road widening;
- Mallanna/Patnam dimensions or pujari guidance are provided.
